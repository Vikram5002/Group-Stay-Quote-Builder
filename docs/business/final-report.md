# Group Stay Quote Builder: final report

Case C32, Fynd x NMIMS Hyderabad. Product and business simulation.

> **Honesty statement.** All data is synthetic. We claim no live revenue, no market demand and
> no production readiness. Business statements are tagged Observed, Derived or Assumption in
> the [evidence ledger](../../evidence/ledger.csv); anything untested is labelled as such.

## 1. Summary

A small hotel's sales manager can now turn a group enquiry into a priced, dated offer in one
step. The system never offers more rooms than exist on every night, holds them while the offer
is open, releases them when it expires, and stops any discount below the contribution floor from
being confirmed until a different owner approves it. It is built, tested (774 automated tests,
all passing) and **deployed on AWS**, where the three public checks pass with real sign-ins.

Whether small hotels have this problem at the size we assume, and whether they would pay, is
**not yet proven**. We define exactly how to test it in the next 30 days (section 7).

## 2. The problem

Group quotes are built in spreadsheets and by memory: answers are slow, rooms can be promised
twice, discounts are given without seeing contribution, and old prices linger. (Status:
hypotheses H1 to H5, [discovery plan](discovery-plan.md).)

## 5. Business case

**Customer:** a small independent hotel, the owner buying and a sales manager using it
(Assumption).

**Value arithmetic (Derived):** one prevented below-floor deal like the rate-1500 case protects
₹6,000 of contribution. A ₹1,999 monthly subscription pays back if that happens about once
every three months; the real frequency is unknown (H2).

**Pricing to test:** a ₹1,999 per-property subscription with a free 30-day shadow pilot,
falling back to a hybrid of a low base fee plus a per-group fee
([pricing](pricing-and-unit-economics.md)).

**Cost to serve (Derived from measured usage):** AWS is under ₹1 per hotel per month at 100
quotes ([cost-to-serve](cost-to-serve.md)); support time dominates. Gross margin at ₹1,999 is
about 80% on the assumed hour of support a month, and break-even is about 2 hotels
([model](model/output.md)). These rest on assumptions until the pilot measures support time.

## 6. Growth experiment

Designed and ready ([growth experiment](growth-experiment.md)): role-play organisers receive
either the tool's dated, itemised offer (generated by the live system) or a typical vague reply,
and we compare response time and outcome tier. **Status: not yet run.** When run, its results
are process evidence from role-play, not market demand.

## 8. Limits

- Synthetic data only; one hotel, one room type in the fixture.
- Business hypotheses H1 to H6 are untested.
- Performance was measured locally; AWS latency and cost need a week of real use.
- Floor semantics await the case owners' answer; the default (absolute) sends small groups to
  owner review.
